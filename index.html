03 / STUDY SYSTEM

Understand first,
remember later.

METHOD 01

Article → Idea

Do not study every Article as just a number. Connect it with its constitutional purpose and meaning.

METHOD 02

Part → Theme

Connect each Part with its main theme. Fundamental Rights, DPSP and institutions become easier to understand and recall.

METHOD 03

Schedule → Detail

Understand Schedules through their practical purpose instead of memorizing only their numbers.

METHOD 04

Amendment → Change

When studying an Amendment, identify what changed in the Constitution and understand its political and constitutional significance.

METHOD 05

Case → Principle

Connect important Supreme Court cases with constitutional doctrines and principles.

METHOD 06

Revision → Recall

Search, open the card, understand the concept and then explain it in your own words without looking at the screen.
